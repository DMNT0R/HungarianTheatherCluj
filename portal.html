(T) Our Portal

Create walking tours for your city

Theatres, museums, libraries, cafés and other institutions can publish their own audio walks in the (T) Our app. Apply with the form below. After your application has been approved, you can log in and build tours with texts, audio files, pictures and locations in English, Hungarian, Romanian, Turkish, Persian, Spanish, Catalan, German and Arabic.

Your application is reviewed by the administrator. You can log in as soon as it has been approved.
